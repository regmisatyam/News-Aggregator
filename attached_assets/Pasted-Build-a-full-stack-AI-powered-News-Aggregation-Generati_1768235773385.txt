Build a full-stack AI-powered News Aggregation & Generation App with the following requirements:

Core Functionality

Every 1 hour, automatically generate the Top 10 latest news articles across categories such as:

World

Technology

Business

Politics

Entertainment

Science

Sports

Fetch trending topics or raw headlines from reliable public news sources (RSS feeds or APIs), then rewrite and expand them using AI so that:

Content is 100% plagiarism-free

Written in simple, easy-to-understand language

Sounds natural, human, and engaging

Avoids robotic or repetitive phrasing

News Writing Rules

Each article must be 400–700 words

Use short paragraphs, sub-headings, and storytelling

Explain why the news matters to a normal person

Avoid jargon unless explained simply

Title Generation (VERY IMPORTANT)

Generate irresistible, ultra-clickable headlines that:

Create curiosity without clickbait lies

Use emotional hooks, power words, or urgency

Make users feel they must click

Examples:

“This One Decision Could Change How Millions Live Starting Tomorrow”

“Nobody Expected This Tech Giant’s Next Move — Here’s What Happened”

“A Silent Shift Is Happening Worldwide, And Almost No One Is Talking About It”

Titles must feel extraordinary, dramatic, and premium, not generic.

Save all generated news articles to a database

Store:

title

category

full content

short summary

generated timestamp

source reference (original headline/source only for internal use)

Prevent duplicate news from being saved

Backend

Use Node.js with Express (or Python with FastAPI)

Use PostgreSQL or MongoDB

Implement a cron job / scheduler to run every hour

API

Fetch latest 10 news

Fetch news by category

Frontend

Simple, clean UI (React preferred)

Rewriting news

Expanding content
